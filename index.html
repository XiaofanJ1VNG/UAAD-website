New Creative Infrastructure

We are drawn to work that tests how we perceive, relate, and live together. Work that crosses disciplines, asks difficult questions, and makes room for play.

From emerging technologies to ecological entanglements and collective futures, we support artists who give unfamiliar ideas a form people can encounter together.
